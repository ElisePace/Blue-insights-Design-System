Labelled AI trust marker with a square colour chip (distinct from the round status dot on `Badge`). Used in the "how this was generated" strip and the risk/bias flag list.

```jsx
<AiChip kind="accountability">Accountability · audit trail</AiChip>
<AiChip kind="consent">Consent · opt-in</AiChip>
<AiChip kind="privacy">Privacy · contains PII</AiChip>
<AiChip kind="bias">Bias monitor · fairness flag on APAC</AiChip>
```

Kinds: `accountability`, `consent`, `privacy`, `transparency`, `risk`, `bias`.
